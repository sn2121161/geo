## Content operations (mandatory)

Before researching, revising, or publishing an article in this site, read and follow
[`CONTENT_STANDARDS.md`](./CONTENT_STANDARDS.md). It is the authoritative system prompt
and operating standard for the 出海合规观察 editorial workflow. Its requirements take
precedence over generic writing preferences, including for future scheduled content work.

Do not publish an article unless its public sources, required frontmatter, GEO structure,
legal disclaimer, and build verification meet that standard.

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.
